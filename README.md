# Lock In Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 26, 2026

Lock In is a local-first activity time-tracking app. This Privacy Policy explains how Version 1.0.0 handles information when you use the Android app.

## Information stored by Lock In

Lock In stores the following information in its SQLite database on your device:

- Activity category names, colors, icons, archive state, and display order.
- Activity sessions, including category, start and end timestamps, duration, status, and any note you add.
- Local app preferences, such as the preferred navigation-rail side on supported tablet layouts.

This information is used only to provide the Timer, History, Calendar, Stats, category-management, and related local app features.

## Information Lock In does not collect or share

Version 1.0.0 does not require an account and does not include Firebase, cloud synchronization, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or a developer-operated server for your activity records. Lock In does not sell, rent, or share your activity records with advertisers or other third parties.

Lock In does not intentionally transmit your categories, sessions, notes, or preferences to us.

## Notifications

On Android 13 and later, Lock In may ask for notification permission after you start a timer. If you allow it, Lock In shows an ongoing active-session notification with the selected category and elapsed time. This notification is generated by your device and can be managed in Android notification settings.

## Android system backup

The Android app currently permits Android system backup. If you enable device or Google backup, Android or Google services may back up Lock In's local app data according to your device, operating-system, and Google-account settings. Lock In does not operate or control that backup service.

Review your device backup settings if you do not want Android system services to back up Lock In data.

## Your choices and deleting local data

You control the data stored in Lock In:

- Edit or permanently delete individual saved sessions in the app.
- Archive or restore categories in the app.
- Remove all Lock In app data through **Android Settings → Apps → Lock In → Storage → Clear storage**, or by uninstalling the app.

Clearing storage or uninstalling removes the app's local database from that device. Android system backups, if enabled, are governed by the applicable Android or Google backup controls.

Because Version 1.0.0 has no Lock In account or developer-operated cloud database, there is no server-side profile or activity history for us to access, export, or delete.

## Security

Lock In relies on your device's operating-system security and stores activity data in the app's private local storage. No method of electronic storage is completely risk-free; protect your device with the security controls available to you.

## Children

Lock In does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If future versions add accounts, cloud services, or other data collection, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Lock In's data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes take effect. Material changes will also be reflected through the in-app Privacy Policy link when available.
